I love reading in my spare time, especially when a book grounds itself in my favourite genres while introducing fresh elements and perspectives. It is hard to put into words, but something about [R.F. Kuang's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._F._Kuang) writing style sort of just works for me. In honour of finishing Katabasis, I decided to rank all the books of hers I have read, from my most to least favourite. I want to stress that I enjoyed all her work, some more then others however! Even though Katabasis sits at number six, it is not because I hated it, it's simply my least favourite overall in her lineup which I've enjoyed to read.

1. [The Poppy War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Poppy_War)
2. [Babel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babel,_or_the_Necessity_of_Violence)
3. [Yellowface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowface_(novel))
4. [The Dragon Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dragon_Republic)
5. [The Burning God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Burning_God)
6. [Katabasis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katabasis_(novel))
